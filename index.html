MacroSource · Enterprise app

Sales Cockpit

Faster order entry for customer service

Reps created sales orders against contracts one at a time across several SAP screens, so 300 orders a day meant 300 separate sessions. Now they enter a contract number, see everything left on it, and create multiple validated orders in one step. The cockpit also handles freight purchasing, deliveries, and a live link to warehouse scales.

~70%less time per order
25+ hrssaved per day at full volume

Purchasing Cockpit

One place for buyers

The Sales Cockpit approach, applied to procurement: bulk ordering against supplier contracts, open PO tracking, delivery recording and supplier performance on one screen. It walks buyers through inter-plant stock transfers that used to take several SAP screens in a strict sequence.

MacroSource · Integration

Kahler Integration Hub

Linking SAP to 30 warehouse sites

Each of 30 sites had its own SAP-to-truck-scale connection, so failures were diagnosed site by site with no shared view. The redesign uses one central hub with a lightweight agent per location. Orders flow to the scales, weights flow back almost immediately, and one dashboard shows every site.
